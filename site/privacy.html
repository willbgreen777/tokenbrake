Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 18, 2026

TokenBrake is built privacy-first: the product runs on your own infrastructure, and we designed the purchase flow to collect as little as possible. This policy explains what is and isn't handled by Akkad Empires ("we", "us").

What TokenBrake never stores

The TokenBrake software runs on your server. It is built so that:

  • Your API keys pass straight through to your AI provider and are not persisted by us.
  • Your prompts and responses are never stored — TokenBrake keeps token counts and dollar amounts, not your content.
  • Your usage numbers live in a database on your own machine. We have no access to them.

No purchase, no accounts

TokenBrake is free and open source. There is no checkout, no license to buy, and no account to create — so there is nothing to collect. We take no names, emails, addresses, card numbers, or payment details of any kind.

How we use it

Because there's no account or checkout, there's no email or personal data to use. We do not sell information, we do not advertise, and we run no third-party advertising trackers on this site.

Third parties

Our website is hosted on Vercel, which processes standard server request logs. That's the extent of it — there's no checkout, no payment processor, and no email provider involved.

Your choices

Providing an email is optional. To have any email we hold deleted, or to ask what we hold, contact us at the address below and we'll handle it promptly.
