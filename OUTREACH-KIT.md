## WHO to target (and where to find them, all public)

**Tier 1 — highest pain, easiest to spot (individual devs / small AI teams):**
- Indie hackers shipping an "AI-powered" app (find them on their own sites, Product Hunt listings, Indie Hackers directory).
- Solo devs running agents or a chatbot (their landing pages say "AI assistant," "AI agent," "chat with…").
- Small AI SaaS with a free tier — they *fear* a single user racking up cost (their pricing page shows a free tier + AI features).

**Tier 2 — bigger tickets, worth a call (companies):**
- Startups on a YC / accelerator batch list that mention AI (public batch directories).
- Companies **hiring LLM / AI engineers** right now — a job post for "LLM," "prompt," "RAG," or "OpenAI API" is a dead giveaway they're running real spend (search job boards: LinkedIn Jobs, Wellfound/AngelList, Indeed).
- Agencies that build AI features for clients (their site says "we build AI/GPT solutions") — they eat the API cost on client projects and hate surprises.

**How to build the actual list:** pick a source above, pull 20–30 names + a contact email (usually
on their site's footer, /contact, or the founder's public email). I can help assemble a starter
list from public sources if you point me at a source — just know it's "likely," not "confirmed."
